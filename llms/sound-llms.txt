# Sound Agriculture

> Sound Agriculture (sound.ag) is an Emeryville, California agriculture company founded in 2013 that sells nature-based crop-nutrition products — SOURCE (a foliar soil activator for nitrogen and phosphorus uptake), BLUEPRINT (an arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi inoculant) and INFLECT — to row-crop growers through independent dealers, and runs an epigenetic on-demand breeding program. It publishes no public API, SDK, developer portal or machine-readable contract. This file was generated by API Evangelist from the company's public website on 2026-09-18; it is not published by Sound Agriculture.
